Welcome!

I'm Leonardo Endrizzi, a Ph.D. student in Economics at the Paris School of Economics (PSE).

My research interests are in the fields of Macroeconomics. In particular, my work is focused on understanding the relation between Macroeconomics and Inequality through the lenses of Heterogeneous Agents Models.

My supervisors are Tobias Broer and Riccardo Cioffi.
