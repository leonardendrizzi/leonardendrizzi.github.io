Welcome!

I'm a Ph.D. candidate at the Paris School of Economics (PSE), supervised by Riccardo Cioffi and Tobias Broer.

My research focuses on macroeconomics, finance and inequality with a particular interest in optimal policy design.

I hold an M.Sc. in Economics from PSE and University Paris 1 Panthéon-Sorbonne, a B.Sc. in Mathematics and a B.Sc. in Economics from the University of Trento.
